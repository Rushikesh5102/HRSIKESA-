# HṚṢĪKEŚA — Company Risk Management & Mitigation

## 1. Risk Matrix & Severity Scoring

- **Owner Agent**: Vighna (QA & Risk Verification)
- **Probability Scale**: 1 (Low) to 5 (Critical / Near Certain)
- **Impact Scale**: 1 (Low) to 5 (Critical / Catastrophic)
- **Severity Score Formula**: `SeverityScore = Probability * Impact`

| Severity Score | Severity Classification | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **20 – 25** | `CRITICAL` | Immediate sovereign notification & mitigation protocol |
| **12 – 19** | `HIGH` | Active mitigation tracking & SLA deadline |
| **6 – 11** | `MEDIUM` | Scheduled review during weekly operations review |
| **1 – 5** | `LOW` | Monitored in risk register |
